Privacy note

A simple approach
to your information.

When you contact Alejandra or request updates, we use the details you provide to respond to you or send the updates you asked for. The forms ask only for the information needed for those purposes.

Messages and subscription requests are delivered through a form service when one is configured. Until then, submitting a form opens a draft in your own email app; the request is only sent when you send that email. We do not sell your information or use invasive tracking on this site.

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have sent, and unsubscribe from updates, by emailing alejandra@aleguzman.info.

Embedded videos are hosted on this site and load when you choose to play them. Links to other sites follow those sites’ privacy practices.
